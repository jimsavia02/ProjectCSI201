# Privacy Policy for Heart Rate Tracking App

This Privacy Policy applies to the Heart Rate Tracking application developed as a Student Academic Project.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect heart rate data, basic profile info, and health metrics synced from your Garmin account via Garmin Connect API.

## 2. How We Use Information

The collected data is strictly used for displaying personal health statistics and heart rate tracking features on our admin dashboard for academic evaluation.

## 3. Data Protection

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information or health data with any third parties.
